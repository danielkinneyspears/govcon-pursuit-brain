# Uniform Contract Format

The Uniform Contract Format (UCF) is the standard organization for negotiated
federal solicitations, defined at FAR 15.204-1. It arranges a solicitation into
four parts and Sections A through M so that offerors and evaluators find the
same kind of content in the same place across procurements.

## Key facts

- **Part I — The Schedule:** Sections A (solicitation form), B (supplies/services
  and prices/costs — the CLINs), C (description/specifications/statement of
  work), D (packaging), E (inspection and acceptance), F (deliveries/performance),
  G (contract administration), H (special contract requirements).
- **Part II — Contract Clauses:** Section I.
- **Part III — List of Documents, Exhibits, and Attachments:** Section J (where
  the SOW/PWS, CDRLs, and QASP usually live).
- **Part IV — Representations and Instructions:** Sections K (representations and
  certifications), L (instructions to offerors), M (evaluation factors).

> [!note]
> Task-order and commercial solicitations often compress or rename sections, but
> the functions of L, M, and C are always present somewhere — find them by
> function, not by section letter.
