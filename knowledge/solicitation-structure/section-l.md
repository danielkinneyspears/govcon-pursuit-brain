# Section L — Instructions, Conditions, and Notices to Offerors

Section L tells the offeror **how to prepare and submit the proposal**: the
volume structure, the section order and headings, what each section must
address, page limits, format rules, and submission mechanics. It is the
authoritative source for proposal structure.

## Key facts

- Section L is the organizing axis of the proposal — the document is physically
  built to it, and evaluators score by following it.
- Section L and [[section-m]] must be read together: L says where to write a
  thing, M says how it is scored. A conflict between them is a candidate
  question for the contracting officer.
- Page limits, format rules, and submission mechanics in Section L are hard
  rules; a proposal can be rejected for violating them.

> [!warning] Section L wins
> Where this package's conventions differ from a specific solicitation's
> Section L, **Section L wins. Always.**
