# Section K — Representations, Certifications, and Other Statements of Offerors

Section K of the [[uniform-contract-format]] is where the offeror **represents
and certifies** required facts: small-business status, qualifications,
prohibited-source compliance, lobbying restrictions, and many others. Most are
completed once in [[sam-gov]] and incorporated by reference; some are
solicitation-specific and must be completed in the proposal.

## Key facts

- Annual reps and certs in SAM.gov cover most generic certifications and are
  pulled in at FAR 52.204-8 (annual representations and certifications) and
  related provisions.
- Solicitation-unique certifications (for example, an OCI representation,
  cybersecurity compliance attestations, or socioeconomic status attestation
  at the time of offer) are completed in the proposal itself.
- Submitting a false Section K representation is both a procurement-integrity
  problem and a potential False Claims Act exposure.
- For [[set-aside-overview|small-business]] set-asides, the offeror must hold the relevant SBA
  certification at the **time of offer and award** — Section K confirms it.

> [!warning] No self-certification for socioeconomic set-asides
> SBA program-specific set-asides ([[8a-program|8(a)]], [[sdvosb]],
> [[wosb-edwosb|WOSB/EDWOSB]], [[hubzone]]) require an active SBA-issued
> certification at the time of offer and award. Self-certification is no
> longer accepted for these programs.
