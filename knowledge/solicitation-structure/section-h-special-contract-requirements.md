# Section H — Special Contract Requirements

Section H of the [[uniform-contract-format]] holds **special contract
requirements** that do not fit the standard clauses in Section I but are
mandatory for the specific procurement. It is the most procurement-specific
section after L and M, and it frequently controls the parts of the work that
carry the most risk.

## Key facts

- Common Section H provisions: [[key-personnel-clause|key personnel]],
  [[organizational-conflict-of-interest|OCI mitigation requirements]],
  [[options|option provisions]] beyond standard, security and clearance
  requirements, [[data-rights|data and IP rights]], unique workforce or place
  of performance constraints, [[limitation-on-subcontracting]] specifics
  beyond the standard clause.
- Section H requirements are mandatory and many are evaluated under
  [[section-m]] — they belong in the [[compliance-matrix]] like any other
  mandatory requirement.
- A non-standard Section H provision is often the silent rejection vector: an
  offeror who misses an unusual H requirement loses without knowing why.
