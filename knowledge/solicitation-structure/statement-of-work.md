# Statement of Work (SOW)

A prescriptive description of the work the contractor will perform: what tasks
to do, the deliverables, and (usually) the methods or steps. The SOW lives in
or as an attachment to [[section-c]] of a [[uniform-contract-format]]
solicitation.

## Key facts

- "Design-the-how" — the SOW typically directs the methods and the deliverables.
- Compare to the [[performance-work-statement]] (outcomes-and-standards, not
  methods) and the [[statement-of-objectives]] (objectives only; the offeror
  proposes the SOW or PWS).
- Each task and deliverable in the SOW is a candidate row in the
  [[compliance-matrix]].
- Often accompanied by a [[cdrl]] (data deliverables) and clauses about
  inspection, acceptance, and place of performance.
