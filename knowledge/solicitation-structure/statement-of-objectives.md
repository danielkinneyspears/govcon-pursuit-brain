# Statement of Objectives (SOO)

A short government document that states the **objectives** of the acquisition
without prescribing the work. The offeror proposes the [[statement-of-work]] or
[[performance-work-statement]] that will achieve those objectives.

## Key facts

- Maximum solution freedom — the SOO frames the problem; the offeror frames the
  solution.
- Common in technology-intensive procurements where the government wants
  industry to bring approach.
- Evaluation often weighs how well the proposed SOW/PWS satisfies each
  objective; map each objective to a proposed-approach element in the
  [[compliance-matrix]].
- A SOO usually keeps Section M tightly focused on the objectives, not on
  prescribed tasks.
