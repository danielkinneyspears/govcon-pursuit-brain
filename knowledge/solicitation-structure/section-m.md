# Section M — Evaluation Factors for Award

Section M tells the offeror **how the government will evaluate the proposal and
decide the award**: the evaluation factors and subfactors, their relative
importance, the evaluation approach, and the basis for award. It defines what
"winning" means for a given procurement.

## Key facts

- **Read Section M first.** Its factors, their order, and their weighting are
  the customer telling the offeror where the award will be decided.
- The government evaluates against the stated factors and subfactors and
  nothing else (FAR 15.305). A proposal earns credit only for what Section M
  asks for.
- Every [[win-theme]] must map to a Section M factor, or it will not be scored.
- The evaluation approach is usually [[best-value-tradeoff]] or [[lpta]], and
  it governs how the proposal should be written and priced.
- Price or cost is always evaluated; [[past-performance]] is normally evaluated
  (FAR 15.304(c)(3)).
