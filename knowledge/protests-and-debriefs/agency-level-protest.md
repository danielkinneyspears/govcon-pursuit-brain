# Agency-Level Bid Protest

A **bid protest** filed with the **contracting activity** (or higher level
within the agency, depending on the agency's procedures) under FAR 33.103.
Used as the **first stop** in some cases and the **only path** in some
low-dollar matters.

## Timeliness

- Same general timeliness rules as GAO: improprieties apparent in the
  solicitation **before** receipt of proposals; other protests within
  **10 days** of when the basis is known or should have been known.
- The CICA stay still applies if the agency receives notice of an agency
  protest **before** award (or, after award, within the same windows that
  apply to GAO).

## Practical considerations

- **Lower cost and faster** than GAO or COFC, but the decision-maker is the
  agency itself (or, on request, an independent reviewer at a level above
  the contracting officer).
- A subsequent GAO protest can be filed within 10 days of knowledge of
  initial adverse agency action; an agency protest does not automatically
  preclude a later GAO or COFC protest.
- Some protests are best raised at the agency level first (e.g., where the
  agency may be receptive to corrective action), others go directly to GAO.
