# Postaward Debrief (FAR 15.506)

The government's **explanation** to an offeror, after the source-selection
decision, of how that offeror's proposal was evaluated and the basis for the
award (FAR 15.506). A preaward debrief is available for offerors excluded from
the competitive range (FAR 15.505).

## Timing

- The offeror must submit a **written request within 3 days** after receiving
  notification of contract award.
- To the maximum extent practicable, the **debriefing should occur within 5
  days** after receipt of the written request.
- An offeror that fails to submit a timely request is generally not entitled
  to a postaward debriefing.

## What the debrief includes (at a minimum)

- The Government's **evaluation of the significant weaknesses or
  deficiencies** in the offeror's proposal.
- The overall **evaluated cost or price** and **technical rating** of the
  successful offeror and the debriefed offeror.
- The overall **ranking** of all offerors, when any ranking was developed.
- A **summary of the rationale** for award.
- For commercial item awards, the make and model of the item to be delivered
  by the successful offeror.
- Reasonable responses to relevant questions about whether source-selection
  procedures contained in the solicitation, applicable regulations, and other
  applicable authorities were followed.

## What the debrief does not include

- **Point-by-point comparisons** of the debriefed offeror's proposal with
  those of other offerors.
- Trade secrets and privileged or confidential manufacturing processes and
  techniques.
- Confidential commercial and financial information of other offerors.
- The names of individuals providing reference information about an offeror's
  past performance.

## Relationship to protests

A timely debrief request triggers the CICA-stay protections — if a
post-award protest is filed within the appropriate window after the debrief,
performance must be suspended (see [[gao-bid-protest]]).

> [!warning] The window is short and strict
> Three days is a hard deadline. A late request can forfeit the debrief.
