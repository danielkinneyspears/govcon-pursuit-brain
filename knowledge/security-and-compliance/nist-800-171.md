# NIST SP 800-171

**NIST Special Publication 800-171** — *Protecting Controlled Unclassified
Information in Nonfederal Systems and Organizations* — is the federal
standard contractors implement to protect [[cui|Controlled Unclassified
Information]] on their own (nonfederal) systems.

## Revision status (2026)

- **Rev. 2** is the version **currently required** by DoD under [[cmmc|CMMC
  2.0 Level 2]] — **110 controls** in **14 control families**.
- **Rev. 3** was finalized **May 14, 2024**. It restructures the families,
  adds three new control families (emphasizing supply-chain security,
  incident response, and countering advanced threats), and tightens several
  controls.
- DoD's transition to Rev. 3 will be through future rulemaking; commentators
  expect enforcement to begin between late 2026 and late 2027. Until then,
  Rev. 2 governs CMMC.

## The Rev. 2 control families

Access Control; Awareness and Training; Audit and Accountability;
Configuration Management; Identification and Authentication; Incident
Response; Maintenance; Media Protection; Personnel Security; Physical
Protection; Risk Assessment; Security Assessment; System and Communications
Protection; System and Information Integrity.

## Companion publication

**NIST SP 800-171A** provides the assessment procedures used to evaluate
each requirement. The current and prior revisions track 800-171.

> [!warning] Verify the active version
> CMMC, DFARS clauses (notably **DFARS 252.204-7012**), and agency policies
> tie to a specific revision of 800-171. Verify the active version before
> a Level 2 self-assessment or contract bid.
