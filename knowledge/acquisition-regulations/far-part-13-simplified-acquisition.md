# FAR Part 13 — Simplified Acquisition Procedures

FAR Part 13 prescribes streamlined procedures for acquisitions at or below the
[[simplified-acquisition-threshold]] (SAT), including micro-purchases at or
below the [[micro-purchase-threshold]].

## Key facts

- The default SAT is **$350,000** (raised from $250,000 effective October 1,
  2025); the MPT is **$15,000** (raised from $10,000). Higher thresholds apply
  to contingency operations and certain defense activities (FAR 2.101).
- Part 13 favors imaginative, simple, and quick procedures over the more
  elaborate Part 15 process for low-dollar buys.
- Subpart 13.2 governs micro-purchases (often paid by Government Purchase
  Card); Subpart 13.3 covers simplified acquisitions above the MPT and at or
  below the SAT.
- A special simplified procedure for **commercial products and services up to
  $7.5 million** (and up to $15 million for certain defense and emergency
  acquisitions) exists at FAR 13.5.
- Small-business reservation: acquisitions of supplies and services with an
  anticipated dollar value above the MPT but not exceeding the SAT are
  automatically reserved for small business (FAR 19.502-2).
