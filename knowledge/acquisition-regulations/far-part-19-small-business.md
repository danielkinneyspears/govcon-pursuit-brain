# FAR Part 19 — Small Business Programs

FAR Part 19 implements the small-business policies of the Small Business Act
in federal acquisition: when acquisitions are set aside for small business
concerns, how the various socioeconomic programs operate, the
limitation-on-subcontracting requirements, and subcontracting plans.

## Key topics

- **Set-aside rule of two** (FAR 19.502-2(b)) — a contracting officer must set
  aside any acquisition with an expected value over the SAT for small
  business when there is a reasonable expectation of receiving offers from
  two or more responsible small business concerns at fair market prices.
- **Automatic small-business reservation** for acquisitions above the
  micro-purchase threshold and at or below the SAT (FAR 19.502-2(a)).
- The socioeconomic set-aside programs implemented in Part 19:
  - [[8a-program|8(a) Business Development]] (Subpart 19.8) — 13 CFR Part 124
  - [[hubzone]] (Subpart 19.13) — 13 CFR Part 126
  - [[sdvosb|Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business]] (Subpart 19.14) —
    13 CFR Part 128
  - [[wosb-edwosb|Women-Owned / Economically Disadvantaged Women-Owned
    Small Business]] (Subpart 19.15) — 13 CFR Part 127
- [[limitation-on-subcontracting]] (FAR 52.219-14) — the rule that limits how
  much of a set-aside contract may go to non-similarly-situated subcontractors.
- [[nonmanufacturer-rule]] (FAR 19.505, 13 CFR § 121.406) — for supply
  contracts, sets when a small business may furnish a product it does not
  manufacture.
- **Subcontracting plans** — required of large primes on contracts over the
  applicable threshold (FAR Subpart 19.7).
