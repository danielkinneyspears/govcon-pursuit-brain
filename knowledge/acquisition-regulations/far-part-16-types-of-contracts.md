# FAR Part 16 — Types of Contracts

FAR Part 16 describes the types of contracts available for use, the principles
for selecting among them, and the procedures for placing orders under
indefinite-delivery contracts.

## Key contract types

- **Fixed-price** (Subpart 16.2) — including [[firm-fixed-price]] (16.202),
  fixed-price with economic price adjustment (16.203), fixed-price incentive
  (16.403), fixed-price level-of-effort (16.207). Contractor bears cost risk.
- **Cost-reimbursement** (Subpart 16.3) — including [[cost-reimbursement-contracts|cost-plus-fixed-fee]]
  (CPFF, 16.306), cost-plus-incentive-fee (CPIF, 16.405-1), cost-plus-award-fee
  (CPAF, 16.405-2). Government reimburses allowable costs and pays a fee.
- **Time-and-materials and labor-hour** (Subpart 16.6) — including
  [[time-and-materials]] (16.601) and labor-hour (16.602).
- **Indefinite-delivery** (Subpart 16.5) — including
  [[idiq|indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity]] (IDIQ, 16.504), requirements
  contracts, and definite-quantity contracts. Orders are placed under
  [[fair-opportunity-far-16-505]] for multiple-award IDIQs.

## Choosing a contract type

Per FAR 16.103, the contracting officer selects the contract type that
results in reasonable contractor risk and provides the contractor with the
greatest incentive for efficient and economical performance. The default for
commercial items is fixed-price; cost-reimbursement requires that the
contractor have an adequate cost-accounting system (FAR 16.301-3).
