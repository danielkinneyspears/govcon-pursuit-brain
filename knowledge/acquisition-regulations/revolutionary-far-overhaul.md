# Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO)

A rewrite of the whole [[far-overview|FAR]] ordered by Executive Order
14275, "Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement." It aims to cut the
FAR back to what statute requires, move non-statutory guidance into
non-binding buying guides, and simplify the text.

## Two phases

1. **Model class deviations (2025–2026).** The FAR Council issued a
   replacement text for each FAR part as a model deviation. Each agency then
   adopted it through its own class deviation memo, on its own date. During
   this phase, the rules that apply depend on the agency.
2. **Rulemaking (2026).** The deviations are being made permanent through
   notice-and-comment rules. The first set of proposed rules (20 parts,
   including Part 52) was published on 2026-06-23 with comments due in
   July. The second set of four proposed rules was published on
   2026-09-18. Four sets are planned in total. The FAR Council expects
   final rules, including the CUI rule, around the end of 2026.

## What this means for pursuits

- **Check the agency's deviation.** For any rule that matters to a pursuit
  (ordering, evaluation, clauses), check whether the buying agency has
  adopted the RFO deviation for that part, and from what date.
- **Citations moved.** Section numbers changed in many parts. For
  example, fair opportunity moved from 16.505 to 16.507 (see
  [[fair-opportunity-far-16-505]]). Solicitations written under the old
  text keep the old numbers.
- **Clauses change at award.** Contracts awarded under the deviation carry
  the revised clauses. Older contracts keep theirs unless modified.
- **Watch the final rules.** Final rules may differ from the deviations.
  Expect a further round of changes when they publish.
