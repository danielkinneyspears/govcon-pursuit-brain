# Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) — Overview

The Federal Acquisition Regulation, codified at 48 CFR Chapter 1, is the
primary regulation governing executive-branch acquisitions of supplies and
services. It is published at [acquisition.gov/far](https://www.acquisition.gov/far).

## Organization

The FAR has 53 parts grouped into eight subchapters. Parts most relevant to
the proposal lifecycle:

| Part | Subject |
|---|---|
| 1 | Federal Acquisition Regulations System |
| 2 | Definitions of Words and Terms |
| 3 | Improper Business Practices (includes [[procurement-integrity-act]]) |
| 4 | Administrative and Information Matters |
| 5 | Publicizing Contract Actions (see [[far-5-203-synopsis]]) |
| 6 | Competition Requirements |
| 7 | Acquisition Planning |
| 9 | Contractor Qualifications (includes [[organizational-conflict-of-interest]]) |
| 12 | Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services |
| 13 | Simplified Acquisition Procedures |
| 15 | Contracting by Negotiation (the source-selection backbone) |
| 16 | Types of Contracts (includes [[fair-opportunity-far-16-505]]) |
| 19 | Small Business Programs |
| 33 | Protests, Disputes, and Appeals |
| 42 | Contract Administration and Audit Services (includes [[cpars]]) |
| 52 | Solicitation Provisions and Contract Clauses |

> [!warning] Being rewritten
> The whole FAR is being rewritten under the [[revolutionary-far-overhaul]].
> Each agency has adopted replacement text for each part by class deviation,
> and proposed rules to make it permanent were published in 2026. Section
> numbers have changed in several parts. Check which text the buying agency
> is using.

## Agency supplements

Many agencies maintain their own supplements that add to (and sometimes
restrict) the FAR — DFARS (Defense), HHSAR (HHS), DEARS / DOEAR (DOE), FARSUP /
AFFARS (Air Force), AGAR (Agriculture), and others. An agency supplement
controls where it adds requirements beyond the FAR.

> [!warning] Regulatory currency
> The FAR and supplements are amended regularly. For any deadline- or
> threshold-sensitive question, verify the current FAR text at
> acquisition.gov/far rather than relying on a wiki summary.
