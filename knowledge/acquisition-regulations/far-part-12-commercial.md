# FAR Part 12 — Acquisition of Commercial Products and Commercial Services

FAR Part 12 establishes the policies and procedures for buying **commercial
products and commercial services** and prescribes streamlined provisions and
clauses consistent with customary commercial practice.

## Key facts

- A "commercial product" or "commercial service" is one sold or offered for
  sale to the general public, or one that has been modified or is of a type
  customarily used by the general public (FAR 2.101 definitions).
- Solicitations use **Standard Form 1449** when the acquisition is expected to
  exceed the [[simplified-acquisition-threshold]] and a paper solicitation or
  contract is being issued (FAR 12.601).
- Subpart 12.6 provides **streamlined procedures** for solicitation,
  evaluation, and award — including the combined synopsis/solicitation
  procedure under FAR 12.603.
- Standard clauses 52.212-1 (Instructions), 52.212-2 (Evaluation), 52.212-3
  (Representations and Certifications), 52.212-4 (Terms and Conditions), and
  52.212-5 (Required Implementing Clauses) are the workhorse provisions.
- The government's strong preference is to acquire commercial items where
  available (10 U.S.C. and 41 U.S.C. preferences carried into FAR).
