# Truthful Cost or Pricing Data (formerly TINA)

The statutory regime requiring offerors to submit and certify **cost or
pricing data** for certain non-competitive negotiations. Formerly known as the
**Truth in Negotiations Act (TINA)**, the statutes were renamed to the
**Truthful Cost or Pricing Data** statutes in the FY2018 NDAA. Implemented in
FAR 15.403.

## When data must be certified

- Required when the contracting officer cannot determine
  [[price-reasonableness]] through price analysis (e.g., inadequate
  competition) **and** the contract value exceeds the statutory threshold.
- **The threshold rises to $10 million** for prime contracts and subcontracts
  entered into after **June 30, 2026**, per the **2026 NDAA § 1804**. Below
  the threshold, certified cost or pricing data is generally not required,
  though data **other than** certified cost or pricing data may still be
  required.

## Exceptions (FAR 15.403-1)

Certified data is not required when:

- The price is based on **adequate price competition**.
- The price is set by law or regulation.
- A **commercial product or commercial service** is being acquired.
- A **waiver** is granted in writing by the head of the contracting activity.
- For modifications, the modification does not change the contract type and
  is exempt under specific provisions.

## What happens if data is defective

If cost or pricing data later proves defective (incomplete, inaccurate, or
not current as of the certification date), the government may seek a
**price reduction** under FAR 52.215-10/-11 and other remedies.

> [!warning] Threshold subject to change
> The certified cost or pricing data threshold is a moving target — verify
> the current threshold against the current statute and the date of contract
> formation.
