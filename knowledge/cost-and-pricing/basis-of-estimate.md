# Basis of Estimate (BOE)

The **narrative and rationale** supporting each element of a proposed cost or
price: why these hours, this labor mix, these ODCs, this escalation. The BOE
is what makes [[cost-realism-analysis]] survivable and
[[price-reasonableness]] defensible.

## What a strong BOE contains

- **Labor hours and mix** — why this level of effort and this blend of labor
  categories, traced to the tasks in the technical approach.
- **Labor rates** — the source of direct and indirect rates (cost accounting
  system, forward pricing rate agreements, current actuals).
- **Escalation** — the basis for year-over-year escalation applied to options.
- **Other direct costs** — why this travel, these materials, this equipment,
  tied to the proposed approach.
- **Subcontractor costs** — how subcontractor pricing was reviewed and
  determined fair.

## Why it matters

- Without a credible BOE, a cost-reimbursement proposal invites an
  unrealistic-cost adjustment in [[cost-realism-analysis]].
- A weak BOE for fixed-price work invites a [[price-reasonableness]] challenge
  and weakens negotiation posture.
- A BOE that reads "based on engineering judgment" with no further rationale
  is the weakest possible — and is a magnet for evaluator skepticism.
