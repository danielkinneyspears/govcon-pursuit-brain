# Price Reasonableness

The government's determination that a proposed price is **fair and
reasonable** — not too high — based on price analysis (FAR 15.404-1(b)).
Required on every contract; contrast with [[cost-realism-analysis]], which
applies primarily to cost-reimbursement work.

## Common price-analysis techniques (FAR 15.404-1(b)(2))

- Comparison of proposed prices received in response to the solicitation
  (adequate competition is the preferred basis).
- Comparison with previously proposed prices and previous government and
  commercial contract prices for the same or similar items.
- Comparison with parametric estimating methods or yardsticks.
- Comparison with published price lists, market prices, and prices for the
  same or similar items.
- Comparison with the **[[igce|independent government cost estimate]]**.
- Comparison with prices obtained through market research.
- Analysis of pricing information provided by the offeror.

## When more is required

If adequate price competition does not exist, the contracting officer may
need cost analysis or **certified cost or pricing data** under
[[truthful-cost-or-pricing-data]].
