# Independent Government Cost Estimate (IGCE)

> [!warning] The concept page is public; actual IGCEs are not
> This page (a domain-wiki concept) is `sensitivity: public`. **Actual IGCE documents** for a specific procurement are **source-selection-sensitive** (see [[procurement-integrity-act]]); offerors must not solicit, accept, or use a real IGCE before public release.

The government's **own estimate** of what the work should cost, prepared
during acquisition planning. The IGCE supports
[[price-reasonableness|price reasonableness]] analysis and informs decisions
about competition, set-asides, and budget.

## Key facts

- Required for construction contracts (FAR 36.203) and customary for other
  procurements; required for cost-reimbursement and most negotiated buys.
- The IGCE is **non-public** and **source-selection sensitive** before award.
  Federal officials are prohibited from disclosing it (see
  [[procurement-integrity-act]]).
- For the offeror, **never solicit, accept, or speculate from** the IGCE
  before public release. Any signal of the IGCE outside legitimate channels
  is a procurement-integrity issue.

> [!warning] Source-selection-sensitive
> The IGCE is one of the canonical examples of information that may **not**
> be sought, accepted, or used by an offeror before award (FAR 3.104).
