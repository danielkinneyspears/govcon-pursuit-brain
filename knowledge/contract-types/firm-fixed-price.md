# Firm-Fixed-Price (FFP)

A contract that provides for a price not subject to any adjustment on the
basis of the contractor's cost experience in performing the contract (FAR
16.202). The contractor bears the full cost risk and keeps any savings.

## Key facts

- **Default contract type** for commercial items and acquisitions where the
  requirement is well-defined and the risk is manageable (FAR 16.103(b)).
- The proposed price is evaluated for [[price-reasonableness]] — not for
  [[cost-realism-analysis]], unless the solicitation specifically calls for
  realism analysis on a fixed-price effort (FAR 15.404-1(d)(3)).
- The contractor proposes a single price (per CLIN); the basis of estimate is
  the contractor's internal concern, although the government may request data
  to support price reasonableness.
- Risk allocation: a contractor who underestimates the work bears the loss; a
  contractor who delivers efficiently keeps the upside.
