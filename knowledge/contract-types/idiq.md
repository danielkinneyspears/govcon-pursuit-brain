# Indefinite-Delivery, Indefinite-Quantity (IDIQ)

A type of indefinite-delivery contract that provides for an indefinite
quantity of supplies or services, within stated limits, during a fixed period
(FAR 16.504). The government places task or delivery orders under it.

## Key facts

- An IDIQ states a **minimum** quantity the government will order (the
  guarantee), and a **maximum** ceiling.
- **Multiple-award IDIQ:** common form — several contractors hold the IDIQ
  and compete for individual orders under [[fair-opportunity-far-16-505|fair
  opportunity]]. Government-wide acquisition contracts (GWACs) and many
  agency vehicles are multiple-award IDIQs.
- **Single-award IDIQ:** less common; the holder receives all orders.
- The IDIQ vehicle is a "hunting license" — winning the IDIQ does not
  guarantee work beyond the minimum; real revenue comes from winning
  individual orders.
- Major federal IDIQs and GWACs include [[gsa-mas|GSA MAS]] (technically a
  Federal Supply Schedule program, but functions similarly), [[oasis-plus]],
  [[polaris]], [[alliant-3]], [[sewp-vi]], and (legacy) [[nitaac-cio-sp]].
