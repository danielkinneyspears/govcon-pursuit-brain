# Time-and-Materials (T&M)

A contract that provides for acquiring supplies or services on the basis of
**direct labor hours at fixed hourly rates** that include wages, overhead,
general and administrative expenses, and profit, plus **materials at cost**
(FAR 16.601).

## Key facts

- Used only when the contracting officer determines no other contract type is
  suitable (FAR 16.601(c)) — a "type of last resort" because cost risk is
  shared and contractor has limited incentive to control hours.
- Each CLIN typically has a not-to-exceed amount; the government pays hours
  used at the fixed rates up to that cap.
- A **ceiling price** is required; the contractor exceeds it at its own risk
  unless the contracting officer authorizes the change in writing.
- A close cousin is **labor-hour** (FAR 16.602), which is the same except
  materials are not furnished by the contractor.
- T&M can be used for commercial services with specific findings (FAR 12.207).
