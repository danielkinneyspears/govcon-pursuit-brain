# Past Performance

The government's assessment of how well an offeror performed comparable prior
work, used to predict performance on the contract being competed. Normally
evaluated as a **performance-confidence assessment** separate from technical
adjectival ratings.

## The three tests

- **Recency** — recent enough to be meaningful (the window is set in Section M;
  commonly within the last 3–5 years from the solicitation date).
- **Relevancy** — similar in scope, magnitude, complexity, contract type,
  customer, and place of performance to the current requirement.
- **Quality** — how well the prior work was performed.

## Quality evidence (strongest first)

- **CPARS** ratings ([[cpars]]).
- **Past Performance Questionnaires (PPQs)** completed by the prior customer.
- **Award-fee** or incentive history.
- **Quantified outcomes** delivered (schedule met, cost saved, targets exceeded).
- **Commendations and awards**.

## Performance confidence ratings

A common scale: **Substantial Confidence / Satisfactory Confidence / Limited
Confidence / No Confidence / Neutral or Unknown Confidence**. Exact scale is
set by the agency procedure or the solicitation.
