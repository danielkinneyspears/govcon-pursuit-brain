# 8(a) Business Development Program

The **8(a) Business Development** program is an SBA-administered program for
small businesses owned and controlled by **socially and economically
disadvantaged** U.S. citizens. Participants can compete for set-aside
contracts and receive sole-source awards within program rules.

## Eligibility

- At least **51% owned and controlled** by U.S. citizens who meet the social
  and economic disadvantage tests.
- **Social disadvantage** — presumed for designated groups; others can
  establish it by narrative.
- **Economic disadvantage** — generally requires personal net worth under
  **$850,000**, average adjusted gross income under **$400,000** (3-year
  average), and total assets under **$6.5 million** (excluding the primary
  residence and the firm).
- The firm must be small under the applicable NAICS
  [[size-standards-and-naics|size standard]] and demonstrate **potential for
  success**.
- **9-year program term**.

## Contracting benefits

- **Sole-source awards** up to specified thresholds (commonly $4.5M services /
  $7M manufacturing for non-Tribal 8(a); Tribal/ANC/NHO-owned firms have
  higher sole-source thresholds, including no statutory ceiling on some).
- **Competitive 8(a) set-asides** above the sole-source thresholds.
- Mentor-protégé joint ventures with non-8(a) mentors are allowed via
  [[sba-mentor-protege]].

> [!warning] SBA certification required
> The firm must hold an active 8(a) certification at the time of offer and
> award. Apply through certify.sba.gov.
