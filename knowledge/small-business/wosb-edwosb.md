# Women-Owned Small Business (WOSB) and Economically Disadvantaged WOSB (EDWOSB)

Programs for small businesses at least **51% owned and controlled by one or
more women**. The **EDWOSB** variant adds an economic-disadvantage test.

## Eligibility

- 51% direct, unconditional ownership and control by one or more women who are
  U.S. citizens.
- The firm must be small under the applicable NAICS
  [[size-standards-and-naics|size standard]].
- For **EDWOSB**, the women owners must also meet economic-disadvantage
  thresholds set by SBA (similar in spirit to the 8(a) thresholds; check
  current limits at certify.sba.gov).
- Set-asides apply only in **NAICS codes designated by SBA** as
  underrepresented (WOSB) or substantially underrepresented (EDWOSB).

## Contracting benefits

- **WOSB and EDWOSB set-asides** in designated NAICS codes.
- **Sole-source authority** within designated NAICS, subject to dollar caps
  (FAR 19.1506).

> [!warning] SBA certification required
> WOSB and EDWOSB self-certification is no longer accepted. Firms must hold
> the SBA certification at the time of offer and award.
