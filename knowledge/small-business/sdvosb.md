# Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB)

A small business at least **51% owned and controlled** by one or more
**service-disabled veterans** recognized by the U.S. Department of Veterans
Affairs (VA). For VA-specific procurements, the firm may also qualify as a
**Veteran-Owned Small Business (VOSB)** under the Vets First Contracting
Program.

## Eligibility

- 51% direct, unconditional ownership and control by one or more
  service-disabled veterans.
- The firm must be **small** under the applicable NAICS
  [[size-standards-and-naics|size standard]].
- Certified by SBA (the SDVOSB certification process consolidated under SBA
  in 2023, replacing prior VA self-certification).

## Contracting benefits

- **SDVOSB set-asides** and **SDVOSB sole-source awards** across the federal
  government (above the SAT, sole-source subject to dollar caps per FAR
  19.1406).
- Within the VA, the **Vets First Contracting Program** gives priority to
  SDVOSBs and VOSBs.

> [!warning] SBA certification required
> SDVOSB self-certification is no longer accepted for set-asides outside the
> VA Vets First program. Hold the SBA certification before the offer date.
