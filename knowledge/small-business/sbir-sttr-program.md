# SBIR and STTR Programs

The **Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR)** and **Small Business
Technology Transfer (STTR)** programs set aside a share of federal
agencies' extramural R&D budgets for small businesses: 3.2% for SBIR and
0.45% for STTR. Awards run in three phases: Phase I (feasibility), Phase II
(development), and Phase III (commercialization with non-SBIR funds,
including sole-source government contracts).

## Status

- Authority **lapsed on 2025-09-30**. No new awards were made during the
  lapse.
- **Reauthorized on 2026-04-13** by the Small Business Innovation and
  Economic Security Act of 2026 (S. 3971), **through 2031-09-30**.

## 2026 changes

- **Per-firm proposal caps** tightened, aimed at firms that submit hundreds
  of proposals a year. Exact numbers are in SBA implementing guidance.
- **Strategic Breakthrough Phase II:** a new award type up to **$30
  million**, limited in number, at agencies with more than $100 million in
  SBIR obligations (DoD, NIH, DOE, NASA, NSF).
- **Backlog authority:** agencies can award proposals identified during the
  lapse on an expedited basis.
- **Expanded foreign-risk screening** of ownership, financial ties, and
  personnel.
- **Phase III training** required for agency contracting staff.
- Agencies can carry unused FY2026 SBIR/STTR funds into FY2027.

## DoD solicitation (FY2026)

DoD issued FY2026 SBIR as the "DoW 2026 SBIR CSO" under 10 U.S.C. 3458
(DFARS 212.70), not a BAA. Monthly releases; Volume 7 foreign-affiliation
disclosures reviewed by the Foreign Risk Evaluation program; a projected
CMMC level on each topic; awards as contracts or Other Transactions
(10 U.S.C. 4021/4022/4023). Evaluators may use approved AI tools for
preliminary tasks (Amendment 3, 2026-09-02).

## Unchanged

- Eligibility: for-profit, US place of business, 500 or fewer employees
  including affiliates, majority US-citizen or permanent-resident ownership
  (with VC-ownership options at participating agencies). See
  [[size-standards-and-naics]].
- Work split: SBIR Phase I at least two-thirds by the small business, Phase
  II at least half. STTR at least 40% small business and 30% research
  institution.
- SBA award size guidelines, adjusted for inflation (most recently about
  $305K Phase I and about $2.05M Phase II). Topics often state their own
  amounts.
