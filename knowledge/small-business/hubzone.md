# HUBZone Program

The **Historically Underutilized Business Zone (HUBZone)** program helps small
businesses **located in** and **employing residents of** HUBZones gain
preferential access to federal contracts.

## Eligibility

- The firm must be a small business under the applicable NAICS
  [[size-standards-and-naics|size standard]].
- At least **51% owned and controlled** by U.S. citizens (or by a Community
  Development Corporation, an agricultural cooperative, an Alaska Native
  Corporation, a Native Hawaiian Organization, or an Indian tribe).
- The **principal office** must be located in a HUBZone.
- At least **35% of employees** must reside in a HUBZone.
- SBA certification required; the HUBZone map is updated periodically and an
  area's designation can change.

## Contracting benefits

- **HUBZone set-asides** and **HUBZone sole-source awards** (subject to
  dollar caps per FAR 19.1306).
- A **10% price evaluation preference** for HUBZone firms in full and open
  competitions (FAR 19.1307).

> [!warning] HUBZone map changes
> Because the HUBZone map is redrawn periodically (currently every five years
> per the 2018 reform, with annual updates for redesignated areas), a firm's
> eligibility can lapse without action by the firm. Track designations.
