# Fair Opportunity (FAR 16.505)

The procedure for placing task or delivery orders under multiple-award
indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity ([[idiq]]) contracts: each awardee
must get a **fair opportunity** to be considered for each order over the
[[micro-purchase-threshold]] (FAR 16.505(b)(1)).

## Key facts

- Above the MPT, each order shall be placed on a competitive basis unless one
  of the exceptions in FAR 16.505(b)(2) applies (sole source justifications
  such as urgency, sole-source follow-on, minimum guarantee, or sole capable
  source).
- The contracting officer provides **fair notice** of the intent to purchase
  to all awardees and affords each a fair opportunity to submit an offer.
- Streamlined procedures: no full FAR Part 15 process. The ordering activity
  decides how robust the competition will be — often Request for Task Order
  Proposal (RTOP) with a compressed Section L / M.
- For orders **above $6 million** under multiple-award contracts (other than
  for advisory and assistance services, where the threshold is lower), the
  procedures of FAR 16.505(b)(1)(iv) apply: notice, statement of work,
  reasonable response period, written evaluation, and a brief explanation of
  the basis for award to unsuccessful awardees.

> [!note]
> Protest rights on task orders are restricted — see
> [[task-order-protest-rights]].
