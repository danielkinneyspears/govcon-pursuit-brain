# Red Team Review

The **source-selection evaluator's review** of a **near-final (~90%) draft**.
The Red Team scores the proposal as a government evaluator would, against
[[section-m]] — finding [[strengths-weaknesses-deficiencies]] before the
government does.

## What it does

- Reads each section as the government evaluator who scores it.
- Assigns [[adjectival-ratings]] for each Section M factor.
- Documents strengths, weaknesses, significant weaknesses, and deficiencies
  — with locations.
- Assigns a [[past-performance|performance-confidence]] rating.
- Gives an **integrated verdict**: would this proposal, as written, win the
  tradeoff (or, under [[lpta]], be the lowest-priced acceptable offer)?

## Discipline

- Score **against Section M**, not reviewer taste.
- **Be the adversary**: a gentle Red Team is worthless. The weaknesses must be
  found now while they can still be fixed.
- Every finding has a **location** and a **specific recommended fix**.
- The team **reviews and prescribes**; it does **not rewrite**. Rewrites belong
  to [[review-recovery]] and the drafting skills.
