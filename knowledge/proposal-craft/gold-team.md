# Gold Team Review

The **executive readiness review** of the **final proposal**, performed in
the days before submission. The Gold Team is the go / no-go.

## What it checks

- Is the proposal **compelling and coherent across volumes** — one voice, one
  story, consistent themes?
- Are the [[red-team]] findings resolved (per [[review-recovery]])?
- Is it **compliant and submission-ready**?
- Final executive judgment: submit, or not.

## What it is not

- Not a Red Team redo — by Gold, scoring decisions are settled; the question
  is readiness.
- Not the place for new findings unless they would actually stop submission;
  the recovery window has closed.

## Output

A readiness verdict and any last must-fix items. Whatever fixes remain feed
the final production cycle and [[submission-compliance-check|the final
submission check]].
