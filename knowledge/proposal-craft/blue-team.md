# Blue Team Review

The **earliest** color review — of the **solution and the win strategy**,
before or during solutioning and before heavy drafting. The Blue Team reviews
the **thinking**, not the writing.

## What it checks

- Is the **solution sound**, and does it deliver the discriminators the win
  strategy claims?
- Are the [[win-theme]]s real (valuable, true, provable, rare) and
  substantiated?
- Does the approach **beat the assessed competition** (read against the
  [[black-hat]])?
- Is the solution **affordable** against the [[price-to-win]] target?

## Why it pays off

A wrong solution caught at Blue costs days of solutioning rework; the same
wrong solution caught at [[red-team]] costs a recovery cycle across drafted
sections. Blue is the cheapest place to be wrong.

## Output

A readiness assessment and findings that correct the **solution and the
strategy** before they are committed to storyboards and drafts. Findings flow
to [[review-recovery]].
