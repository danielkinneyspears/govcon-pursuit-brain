# Pink Team Review

The early proposal review — on **storyboards and an early draft (~30–50%
complete)** — focused on **strategy and structure**, not polished prose.

## What it checks

- Does the structure follow [[section-l]]?
- Does every section have a clear theme tied to a customer hot button and a
  [[section-m]] factor?
- Is the compliance approach sound — is every requirement assigned a home and
  on track to be answered?
- Do the storyboards, read together, tell one coherent, winning story?
- Are proof points identified for the key claims?

## What it does not check

Polished prose, voice, graphics. The Pink Team is too early for those; fixing
them now is wasted effort if the strategy or structure changes.

## Output

A readiness assessment and findings that redirect drafting **before** heavy
writing begins. Findings flow to [[review-recovery]].
