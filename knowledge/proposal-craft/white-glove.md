# White Glove (Book Check) Review

The **final production / book check** of the **assembled submission
package**, after [[gold-team]] and before submission. A meticulous,
page-by-page human pass.

## What it checks

- Is every volume **complete and correctly assembled**?
- Is formatting **consistent** — headings, numbering, graphics, captions,
  fonts, page numbers?
- Are there **production defects** — broken cross-references, missing pages,
  misplaced graphics, bad page breaks, low-resolution images?
- Are file **names, formats, and the package structure** correct?

## Relationship to submission compliance

The White Glove is the **human quality pass** over the produced artifact.
Substantive conformance against the solicitation's rules (page limits,
required forms, volume separation) is the job of
**submission-compliance checking**, which verifies the rules in [[section-l]]
and elsewhere. Both run before submission; they catch different things.
