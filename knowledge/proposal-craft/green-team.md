# Green Team Review

The **cost/price volume review**, performed once a cost model and the
technical and management volumes exist. The Green Team reviews the pricing
the way the government's cost evaluators — and the offeror's own management —
will.

## What it checks

- Is the **pricing strategy** sound and consistent with the
  [[price-to-win]] posture?
- Is the **[[basis-of-estimate]]** sufficient and traced to the technical
  approach?
- Is the cost **realistic** — neither padded nor implausibly low (
  [[cost-realism-analysis]])?
- Is the fee/profit posture appropriate to the contract type and competitive
  posture?
- Is the cost volume **consistent with the technical and management
  volumes** (the cost-technical consistency check)?
- Is the cost volume **compliant** with the Section L cost instructions and
  the Section B [[clin]] structure?

## Output

Findings on pricing strategy, BOE sufficiency, realism, cost-technical
consistency, and cost-volume compliance — before the cost volume is finalized.
Findings flow to [[review-recovery]].
