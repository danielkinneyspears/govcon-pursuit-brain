# Color Team Review

A staged proposal review, named by color, that examines the proposal through a
**defined lens at a defined maturity**. Reviews catch problems while they are
still cheap to fix. The exact set varies by company; this package treats six
as canonical, plus the [[black-hat]] (a capture-phase competitive simulation,
not a proposal review).

## The reviews

| Review | Maturity | Lens |
|---|---|---|
| [[blue-team]] | Solution / win strategy | Right offer? |
| [[pink-team]] | ~30–50% draft | Strategy, structure, themes |
| [[green-team]] | Cost / price volume | Pricing strategy, BOE, realism |
| [[red-team]] | ~90% draft | The source-selection evaluator's view |
| [[gold-team]] | Final | Executive go/no-go |
| [[white-glove]] | Assembled package | Production / book check |

## The findings discipline

Every finding from any review is recorded with:

- **Location** — volume, section, requirement ID.
- **Finding** — what is wrong.
- **Severity** — deficiency, significant weakness, weakness, strength
  opportunity, or administrative (see [[strengths-weaknesses-deficiencies]]).
- **Recommended fix.**

A "finding" without a location and a fix cannot be worked in recovery; do not
record it that way. A review whose findings are not worked is theater.
