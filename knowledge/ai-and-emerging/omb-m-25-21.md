# OMB M-25-21 — Accelerating Federal Use of AI Through Innovation, Governance, and Public Trust

OMB Memorandum **M-25-21** (April 3, 2025) is the current government-wide
policy on federal **use** of artificial intelligence. It **rescinded and
replaced M-24-10** (the prior Biden-era policy).

## Core themes

The memo directs executive-branch agencies to accelerate federal AI use by
prioritizing three themes:

1. **Innovation** — remove unnecessary barriers to agency adoption of AI.
2. **Governance** — manage AI risk through enterprise AI strategies, named
   officials, inventories, and minimum practices.
3. **Public trust** — preserve transparency and accountability in agency use
   of AI.

## What it requires of agencies

- **Adopt enterprise AI strategies**.
- **Designate Chief AI Officers (CAIOs)** at the agency level.
- **Maintain public AI use-case inventories**.
- Apply **minimum risk-management practices** for **"high-impact" AI** —
  systems that could materially affect rights, safety, or significant
  benefits.

## Relationship to NIST

Unlike M-24-10, M-25-21 does **not specifically require** use of the
**NIST AI Risk Management Framework**. Agencies are still free to use it; the
memo just does not mandate it. Verify what current solicitation language
requires before assuming NIST AI RMF applies.
