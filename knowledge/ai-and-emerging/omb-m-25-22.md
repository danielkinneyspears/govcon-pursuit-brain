# OMB M-25-22 — Driving Efficient Acquisition of AI in Government

OMB Memorandum **M-25-22** (April 3, 2025) provides government-wide guidance
on the **efficient and secure acquisition** of AI systems and services. It
**rescinded and replaced M-24-18** (the prior Biden-era acquisition memo).

## Core themes

1. **A competitive American marketplace for AI** — favor open, competitive
   procurement to ensure quality and cost-effectiveness.
2. **Safeguarding taxpayer dollars** — track AI performance and manage risk
   throughout the contract lifecycle.
3. **Cross-functional engagement** — pair acquisition teams with technical
   AI expertise.

## Practical implications for federal contractors

- AI vendors should expect **performance-tracking** and **risk-management**
  requirements written into solicitations and contracts, even where the
  underlying contract is otherwise commercial.
- Expect more **terms and conditions specific to AI** in vehicles such as
  [[gsa-mas]] (Refresh 31 introduces AI-related terms) and agency
  solicitations.
- The memo emphasizes **competition**; solicitations may discourage
  sole-source AI procurements absent strong justification.

## Relationship to NIST

Like its companion [[omb-m-25-21]], M-25-22 does **not specifically require**
use of the NIST AI RMF. Agencies retain discretion; some solicitations will
still reference NIST guidance.
