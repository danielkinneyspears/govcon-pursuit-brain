# Key Personnel Clause

A contract clause — most often in **Section H** (special contract requirements)
of a [[uniform-contract-format|UCF]] solicitation — that names individuals
the contractor commits to provide for performance and restricts substitution
without government approval.

## Typical provisions

- **Named individuals** by position (e.g., Program Manager, Service Desk
  Lead). The clause incorporates the proposed resumes from the proposal.
- **Period of dedication** — usually the base period or a stated minimum
  period of performance.
- **Substitution rules** — substitutions must be requested in writing in
  advance; the proposed substitute must meet or exceed the original's
  qualifications; the contracting officer must approve.
- **Notice periods** — common requirement of 14–30 days' advance notice for
  any change to key personnel.

## Proposal implications

- Each named key person's **resume** is evaluated against the position's
  stated qualifications under [[section-m]].
- The team must propose only **realistically available** people. Naming
  strong personnel with no real intent or ability to deliver them ("bait
  and switch") is an integrity exposure and a performance risk.
- **Letters of commitment** or contingent-hire agreements support
  availability where the individual is not a current employee.
