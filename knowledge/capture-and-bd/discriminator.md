# Discriminator

A **strength that gives the evaluator a reason to choose this offer over the
others**. Discriminators are the basis of a real [[win-theme]]; without one,
a theme is empty.

## The four-part test

A strength is a discriminator only if **all four** hold:

1. **Valuable** — the customer cares about it (it ties to a hot button or a
   Section M factor).
2. **True** — the offeror can genuinely deliver it.
3. **Provable** — there is evidence: past performance, certifications,
   measurable results.
4. **Rare** — competitors cannot readily claim the same thing.

A strength that is valuable, true, and provable but **not rare** is **table
stakes** — necessary to state for compliance, but not a theme. Mistaking
table stakes for discriminators is the most common win-strategy error.

## The rarity test

If the competitor across the street can say the same sentence, the strength
discriminates nothing. Discriminators must be validated against the
competitive field — they cannot be judged without knowing what competitors
offer.
