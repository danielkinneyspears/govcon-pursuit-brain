# Polaris (GSA Small Business IT GWAC)

GSA's **small business IT** government-wide acquisition contract (GWAC),
enabling federal agencies to set IT task orders aside for small business
categories.

## Structure (as of 2026)

- Multiple-award IDIQ; small-business-only.
- **Pools** by socioeconomic category: Small Business, WOSB, SDVOSB,
  HUBZone — agencies set aside an order to a specific pool.
- GSA has been issuing rolling rounds of awards across the small-business and
  socioeconomic pools through Fiscal Year 2026, with more pool awards
  expected.

## What it covers

- IT services (services in support of IT, including emerging technology)
  across federal civilian and DoD customers.
