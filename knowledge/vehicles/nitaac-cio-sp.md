# NITAAC CIO-SP3 / CIO-SP4 (Cancelled)

The National Institutes of Health Information Technology Acquisition and
Assessment Center (NITAAC) operates IT GWACs used government-wide. The
follow-on **CIO-SP4** was **cancelled** in 2026; **CIO-SP3** remains active
under extension.

## Status (as of 2026)

- **CIO-SP4 cancelled** by NITAAC based on stated goals to reduce vehicle
  duplication. Protests had delayed the procurement for roughly four years.
- **CIO-SP3 has been extended** multiple times; extension reported through
  April 2026, with potential further extensions while consolidation plans
  proceed.
- GSA has been negotiating to assume the NITAAC IT GWAC mission under its
  management as part of consolidation with [[gsa-mas]], [[alliant-3]],
  [[polaris]], and [[sewp-vi]].

> [!warning] Vehicle landscape changing
> Pursuit and teaming decisions tied to CIO-SP4 are obsolete. Plans that
> depended on CIO-SP4 need a refresh against the current IT GWAC landscape
> (Alliant 3, SEWP VI, Polaris, MAS).
