# Alliant 3

GSA's large-business IT government-wide acquisition contract (GWAC), the
successor to Alliant 2. Used by federal agencies for complex IT services
acquired through task orders.

## Status (as of 2026)

- **No contract ceiling** (per GSA's Alliant 3 program page).
- **Phase I Notice to Proceed issued March 10, 2026.** Per GSA: "The contract
  is now live, and orders can be placed against it."
- Multiple-award IDIQ; large-business; orders compete under
  [[fair-opportunity-far-16-505]].

> [!note] Earlier reports of a ceiling
> Some third-party trade-press reporting referenced a multi-billion-dollar
> ceiling figure for Alliant 3 during the source-selection period; GSA's
> official program page lists no ceiling as of mid-2026. Always cite the GSA
> page over secondary reporting.

## What it covers

- IT services and IT-related services across the federal government; broad
  scope including infrastructure, software, cybersecurity, and emerging
  technologies.
