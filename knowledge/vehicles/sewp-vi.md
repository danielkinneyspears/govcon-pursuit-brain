# NASA SEWP VI

NASA's Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement, **sixth generation** — a
government-wide IT products and product-based services IDIQ used across
federal agencies.

## Status (as of 2026)

- **SEWP VI awards delayed to mid-2026.**
- Planned **significant expansion** of prime contractors: from ~147 on SEWP V
  to a target of approximately **1,000** primes on SEWP VI.
- GSA has been negotiating to assume SEWP and other major IT GWACs under its
  management as part of a broader vehicle consolidation.

## What it covers

- IT products (hardware, software) and IT product-based services across
  federal civilian and DoD customers.

> [!warning] Vehicle status is fluid
> SEWP VI award timing, the exact pool structure, and GSA consolidation plans
> have been moving targets through 2025–2026. Confirm current status before
> teaming or pursuit decisions.
