# OASIS+ (One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services Plus)

GSA's successor to the original OASIS suite — a family of multiple-award
[[idiq|IDIQ]] contracts for **government-wide professional services**.

## Structure (as of 2026)

- OASIS+ Phase II added domains; the program now spans **13 domains** including
  Management Consulting, Technical and Engineering, Scientific Research,
  Logistics, Environmental, Financial, and others.
- Multiple-award IDIQ; orders compete under
  [[fair-opportunity-far-16-505|fair opportunity]].
- Has small business set-aside pools (e.g., 8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB, HUBZone),
  enabling agencies to set order-level set-asides.
- Phase II rolling apparent-awardee announcements began on SAM.gov on May 12,
  2026 with formal awards and notices to proceed planned on or about May 22,
  2026.

## Ordering

- Agencies may direct-award task orders to small-business pool holders or
  compete across awardees in the relevant domain.
- Ordering activity manages the order-level competition; OASIS+ is the
  contract vehicle.
