# GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS)

The **GSA Multiple Award Schedule** (also "MAS" or "Schedules") is GSA's
consolidated long-term contracting program for commercial supplies and
services, used government-wide. Ordering against MAS uses the Federal Supply
Schedule procedures of FAR Subpart 8.4 (not the full FAR Part 15 process).

## Structure

- **One consolidated MAS contract** replaced the 24 legacy GSA schedules.
- Organized into **12 large categories** with subcategories and Special
  Item Numbers (SINs). SINs are added, merged, and retired through
  refreshes. Check the current SIN list in the solicitation.
- A **SIN** is the classifier under which a contractor's products or
  services are listed and ordered.

## Ordering and competition

- Buyers may order via **GSA eBuy** or directly. For orders above the
  micro-purchase threshold, FAR 8.405 generally requires posting an RFQ to
  eBuy or providing it to as many MAS contractors as practicable. FAR Part 8
  is also being rewritten under the [[revolutionary-far-overhaul]]. Check
  the ordering agency's deviation.
- Schedule pricing is pre-negotiated as **fair and reasonable**, simplifying
  price determinations.

## 2026 program changes

- **Refresh 31 (April 2026)**, implementing GSA Class Deviation 2026-02:
  - **Transactional Data Reporting (TDR) is mandatory for all SINs.**
    Reports are monthly, due 30 days after month end.
  - **Commercial Sales Practices (CSP-1) disclosures and Most Favored
    Customer information are no longer required.**
  - **The old Price Reductions Clause is removed** and replaced by a
    version consistent with TDR. Old-clause liability ends at the start of
    the reporting quarter after the contractor accepts the TDR mod.
  - Existing holders got Mass Mod A909 plus a TDR modification, with 90
    days to accept.
- **Refresh 32 (June 2026)**, Mass Mod A917: implements EO 14398 (DEI
  policies), adds rules for joint venture offers and End-of-Support
  products, and **cuts the mass mod acceptance window to 30 days**.
- **Refresh 33 (scheduled October 2026):** includes three new subgroups
  under SIN 517312 Wireless Mobility Solutions.
- Standing changes: EPA clauses consolidated into GSAR 552.238-210; the
  Order-Level Materials cap removed; price proposals moving to the FAS
  Catalog Platform.
- GSA has been consolidating other vehicles (NASA SEWP, NITAAC IT GWACs)
  under its management.
