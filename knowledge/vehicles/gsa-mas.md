# GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS)

The **GSA Multiple Award Schedule** (also "MAS" or "Schedules") is GSA's
consolidated long-term contracting program for commercial supplies and
services, used government-wide. Ordering against MAS uses the Federal Supply
Schedule procedures of FAR Subpart 8.4 (not the full FAR Part 15 process).

## Structure (as of 2026)

- **One consolidated MAS contract** replaced the 24 legacy GSA schedules.
- Organized into **12 large categories** (Facilities, Furniture and
  Furnishings, Human Capital, Industrial Products and Services, Information
  Technology, Miscellaneous, Office Management, Professional Services,
  Scientific Management and Solutions, Security and Protection, Transportation
  and Logistics Services, Travel) and approximately **82 subcategories** and
  **~328 Special Item Numbers (SINs)**.
- A **SIN** is the classifier under which a contractor's products or services
  are listed and ordered.

## Ordering and competition

- Buyers may order via **GSA eBuy** or directly. For orders above the
  micro-purchase threshold, FAR 8.405 generally requires posting an RFQ to
  GSA's eBuy or providing the RFQ to as many MAS contractors as practicable.
- Schedule pricing is pre-negotiated as **fair and reasonable**, simplifying
  price determinations.

## Recent program changes

- **Refresh 31** (proposed 2026) includes making **transactional data
  reporting** mandatory and adds AI-related terms and conditions; details
  evolve in subsequent refreshes.
- GSA has been consolidating other vehicles (NASA SEWP, NITAAC IT GWACs)
  under its management.
