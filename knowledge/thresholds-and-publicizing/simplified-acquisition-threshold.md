# Simplified Acquisition Threshold (SAT)

The **Simplified Acquisition Threshold** is the dollar ceiling for the
**simplified acquisition procedures** of FAR Subpart 13.3 — streamlined
methods preferred for low-dollar acquisitions to reduce administrative burden.

## Current threshold (effective October 1, 2025)

- **$350,000** — the standard SAT (raised from $250,000 by inflation
  adjustment).

## Higher thresholds in special situations (FAR 2.101)

- **$1,000,000** — acquisitions to support contingency operations or to
  facilitate defense against certain attacks (raised from $800,000).
- **$2,000,000** — certain contingency / defense acquisitions outside the
  United States (raised from $1.5 million).
- **$650,000** — humanitarian or peacekeeping operations (raised from
  $500,000).

## What it triggers

- At or below the SAT (and above the [[micro-purchase-threshold|MPT]]),
  simplified procedures (FAR 13.3) apply: streamlined competition, often
  oral solicitations, no formal certifications beyond the standard reps
  in [[sam-gov]].
- The **rule of two** for small-business set-asides applies above the SAT
  (FAR 19.502-2(b)) — set aside when two or more responsible small
  businesses are expected to offer at fair market prices.
- **Automatic small-business reservation** for acquisitions above the MPT
  and at or below the SAT (FAR 19.502-2(a)).

## A related, special SAT

For acquisitions of **commercial products and services** under FAR 13.5, a
**special simplified procedure** is available up to **$7.5 million** (and up
to $15 million for certain defense and emergency commercial buys). This is
separate from the standard SAT.

> [!warning] Verify current threshold
> Thresholds are inflation-adjusted periodically. Confirm the current SAT
> against FAR 2.101 before relying on it for a date-sensitive procurement.
