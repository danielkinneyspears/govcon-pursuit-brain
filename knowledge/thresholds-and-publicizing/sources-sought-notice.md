# Sources Sought Notice

A **presolicitation notice** the government posts to **identify capable
sources** for a planned acquisition. Sources sought notices feed
[[far-part-19-small-business|small-business set-aside decisions]]: the
government is partly asking, "are there enough capable small businesses to
justify setting this aside?"

## Why respond

- **Shape the set-aside decision** — if enough qualifying small businesses
  respond, the acquisition is more likely to be set aside, which is to a
  small business's advantage.
- **Demonstrate capability** — concise, relevant capability statements help
  the government scope the acquisition realistically.
- **Begin or continue customer engagement** — a sources-sought response can
  open a path to legitimate pre-RFP shaping.

## What to include in a response

Match exactly what the notice asks for. Typically:

- Firm name, UEI, CAGE, NAICS, size, socioeconomic status (truthfully stated).
- Brief, relevant capability and past performance (not a proposal).
- Vehicle holdings (e.g., [[gsa-mas]], [[oasis-plus]], [[polaris]]).
- Constructive feedback on the contemplated approach, where the notice invites
  it.

## Related notices

- **Request for Information (RFI)** — broader market-research instrument; may
  ask for input on approach, technologies, or pricing structures.
- **Draft RFP** — released for industry comment on scope, Section L, and
  Section M; the highest-value shaping vehicle.
- **Industry day** — in-person or virtual session for capability briefings
  and questions.
