# FAR 5.203 — Publicizing Synopses

FAR 5.203 prescribes the time periods for publicizing proposed contract
actions on [[sam-gov]] before issuing a solicitation, the procedures for
combined synopsis/solicitation, and the rules for synopsizing sole-source
actions.

## Time periods

- The synopsis must normally be published at least **15 days** before
  issuance of a resulting solicitation, **or** a sole-source action under the
  authorities of FAR 6.302.
- For acquisitions of **commercial products or commercial services**, the
  contracting officer may **establish a shorter period** or use the
  **combined synopsis/solicitation** procedure (FAR 5.203(a)).
- After the solicitation issues, the response period is independently set in
  the solicitation; for non-commercial, typically at least **30 days** to
  receive proposals (FAR 5.203(c)).

## Types of presolicitation notices

- **Synopsis of a proposed contract action** (the standard presolicitation
  notice).
- **Combined synopsis/solicitation** for commercial buys (FAR 12.603) — the
  notice and the solicitation are issued together.
- **Sources sought / RFI** (presolicitation market research) — not the same
  as a synopsis but commonly used to inform acquisition planning. See
  [[sources-sought-notice]].

## Where notices appear

Most notices are posted on **[[sam-gov]]** (the successor to FedBizOpps);
some agencies maintain supplementary portals. SAM.gov is the legal record.
