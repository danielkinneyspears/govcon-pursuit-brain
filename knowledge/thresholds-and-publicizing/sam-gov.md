# SAM.gov (System for Award Management)

**SAM.gov** is the federal government's official portal for **contractor
registration** and for **publicizing contract opportunities**. A federal
contractor must be **actively registered** in SAM to receive an award and
must keep the registration current.

## What SAM does

- **Entity registration** — provides the offeror's
  **Unique Entity Identifier (UEI)** (replaced the legacy DUNS number in
  2022), CAGE code, banking, points of contact, NAICS codes, certifications.
- **Annual representations and certifications** — most generic reps and
  certs (FAR 52.204-8) are completed in SAM and pulled into proposals by
  reference (see [[section-k-representations-and-certifications]]).
- **Contract opportunities** — successor to FedBizOpps; presolicitation
  synopses (per [[far-5-203-synopsis]]), solicitations, amendments, sources
  sought, draft RFPs, awards, and notices.

## UEI and CAGE

- **UEI** — a 12-character alphanumeric identifier issued by SAM; **does not
  expire**.
- **CAGE Code** — a 5-character DoD-issued code identifying the entity at a
  specific physical address; issued automatically as part of SAM
  registration.

## Active registration is required

- **Annual renewal** — registration must be renewed at least once a year
  (or any time entity information changes).
- An **expired registration** can prevent award and may affect existing
  contracts; track renewal dates and act early.

> [!warning] No SAM, no award
> A federal contracting officer cannot award to an entity without an active
> SAM registration. Verify the registration is current well before the
> proposal due date.
