# Final Proposal Revision (FPR)

After discussions are complete, the contracting officer issues a request for
final proposal revisions (FAR 15.307). The offerors in the
[[competitive-range]] submit FPRs by the stated deadline; the FPR is the
proposal the government evaluates for the [[source-selection-decision]].

## Key facts

- The request for FPRs gives all remaining offerors a common cutoff date and
  time for submission of final proposal revisions.
- The FPR can change material aspects of the proposal that were addressed in
  [[discussions-clarifications-communications|discussions]]; offerors are
  expected to fix the weaknesses and deficiencies the government raised.
- Late FPRs are governed by the late-submission rules (FAR 15.208).
- After FPRs, the source-selection authority makes the
  [[source-selection-decision]].
