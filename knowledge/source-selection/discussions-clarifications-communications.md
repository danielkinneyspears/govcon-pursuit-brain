# Discussions, Clarifications, and Communications

Three escalating, regulated forms of exchange between the government and
offerors during evaluation (FAR 15.306). Which one is taking place changes
what may be exchanged and whether a proposal can be revised.

## The three exchanges

- **Clarifications** (FAR 15.306(a)) — limited exchanges, generally before
  establishing the competitive range. The offeror may explain or clarify
  certain aspects of its proposal but **cannot revise it**.
- **Communications** (FAR 15.306(b)) — exchanges between the government and
  offerors after receipt of proposals but **before establishing the
  [[competitive-range]]**, addressing past-performance information adverse to
  the offeror or proposals that should be in the competitive range. They may
  not change the proposal substantively.
- **Discussions** (FAR 15.306(d)) — exchanges held with offerors in the
  competitive range, intended to maximize the government's ability to obtain
  best value. Discussions **may result in revised proposals**. The contracting
  officer must, at a minimum, indicate to each offeror in the range the
  significant weaknesses, deficiencies, and other aspects of its proposal that
  could be altered or explained to materially enhance the proposal's potential
  for award.

> [!note]
> The government often communicates discussion items as "Evaluation Notices"
> (ENs) — written items identifying a specific weakness, deficiency, or
> uncertainty for the offeror to address.
