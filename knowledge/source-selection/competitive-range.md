# Competitive Range

The set of the most highly rated proposals with which the contracting officer
intends to hold [[discussions-clarifications-communications|discussions]]
(FAR 15.306(c)). Only offerors in the competitive range remain in the
competition through discussions and the final proposal revision.

## Key facts

- The competitive range is established by the contracting officer based on
  the ratings of each proposal against the [[section-m]] factors.
- For efficiency, the range may be limited to the greatest number that will
  permit an efficient competition, after notice in the solicitation.
- Offerors excluded from the competitive range are eliminated from further
  consideration and may request a debrief (preaward, FAR 15.505, or
  [[debrief-far-15-506|postaward]] depending on timing).
- Establishing a competitive range is permitted but not required. An agency
  may award on initial proposals without discussions.
