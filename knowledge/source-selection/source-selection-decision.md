# Source Selection Decision

The decision by the Source Selection Authority (SSA), normally documented in
the Source Selection Decision Document (SSDD), selecting the offer that
represents the best value to the government against the
[[evaluation-factors-and-subfactors|stated evaluation factors]] (FAR 15.308).

## Key facts

- The SSA makes an **independent integrated assessment** based on the
  underlying evaluations and the relative importance of the factors. The SSA
  is not bound to the evaluators' or the SSAC's recommendation but must
  document the rationale.
- For [[best-value-tradeoff]], the SSDD documents the tradeoff: where the
  proposals stood across factors, why any price premium is worth the non-price
  merit (or vice versa), and the basis for award.
- For [[lpta]], the SSDD documents acceptability for each offeror and the
  selection of the lowest-priced acceptable offer.
- The SSDD is the document an offeror later asks about in a
  [[debrief-far-15-506|debrief]] and that may be reviewed in a protest.
