# Best-Value Tradeoff

A source-selection approach in which the government may pay a price premium
for superior non-price merit (FAR 15.101-1). The award goes to the offer that
represents the best value, weighing price against the non-price factors.

## Key facts

- **Strengths have cash value.** Exceeding a requirement in a way that
  benefits the government can justify a higher price.
- The size of the defensible price premium depends on how [[section-m]] weights
  price against the non-price factors. When non-price factors are
  "significantly more important than price," a real [[discriminator]] can
  justify a meaningful premium.
- Proposals should be written to *win the tradeoff* — to show value, not
  merely compliance. This is where a [[win-theme]] earns its premium.
- Contrast with [[lpta]], where exceeding the requirement earns nothing.
