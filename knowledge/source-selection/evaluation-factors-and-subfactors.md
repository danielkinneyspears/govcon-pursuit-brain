# Evaluation Factors and Subfactors

The dimensions on which the government evaluates a proposal, stated in
[[section-m]] of the solicitation and selected in accordance with FAR 15.304.

## Key facts

- The contracting officer selects the factors and subfactors that are relevant
  to the acquisition and represent the key areas of importance the
  source-selection authority will consider.
- **Price or cost must be evaluated** in every source selection.
  **Past performance must be evaluated** in all competitive negotiated
  acquisitions over the simplified acquisition threshold, unless the
  contracting officer documents that it is not appropriate (FAR 15.304(c)).
- The relative order of importance of the factors must be stated. Common
  formulations: "non-price factors significantly more important than price,"
  "approximately equal," "price more important than non-price."
- Subfactors decompose a factor (e.g., a Technical factor with subfactors for
  Approach, Personnel, and Past Performance) and inherit the factor's relative
  weighting.
