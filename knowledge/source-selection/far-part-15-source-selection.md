# FAR Part 15 Source Selection

The process by which the government evaluates negotiated proposals and selects
an awardee, governed by FAR Part 15, Subpart 15.3.

## Key facts

- The arc: solicitation released → proposals received → evaluation against
  [[section-m]] factors (FAR 15.305) → [[competitive-range]] established if
  discussions will be held (FAR 15.306) → discussions → final proposal
  revisions → [[source-selection-decision]] (FAR 15.308) → award →
  [[debrief-far-15-506|debriefs]] (FAR 15.506).
- Evaluation is against the stated factors and subfactors only. Evaluators
  document [[strengths-weaknesses-deficiencies]] and assign
  [[adjectival-ratings]].
- [[discussions-clarifications-communications|Discussions]] can materially
  change a proposal; clarifications and communications cannot.
- An agency may award on initial proposals without discussions if the
  solicitation so states — assume the initial proposal may be the only proposal.
- Task orders under multiple-award IDIQ contracts use
  [[fair-opportunity-far-16-505]] instead of the full Part 15 process.
