# Lowest-Price Technically Acceptable (LPTA)

A source-selection approach in which non-price factors are evaluated pass/fail,
and among the proposals rated technically acceptable, the lowest price wins
(FAR 15.101-2).

## Key facts

- Exceeding a requirement earns no credit and only raises price. Proposals
  should be written to be cleanly *acceptable* at the lowest defensible price —
  no gold-plating.
- The realism floor still applies: a price so low the offer cannot credibly
  meet the acceptability standard fails the technical gate.
- A [[win-theme]] built on exceeding requirements does not help under LPTA;
  the relevant discriminator is being solidly acceptable at a winning price.
- Contrast with [[best-value-tradeoff]], where added value can justify added
  price.
- Many evaluations are hybrids — some factors pass/fail, some tradeoff.
  Identify the approach for each factor from [[section-m]].
