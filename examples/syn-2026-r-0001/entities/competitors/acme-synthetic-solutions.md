## Assessed offer (Black Hat — bid as them)

- **Would they bid?** Yes — defending base; must-win.
- **Likely solution:** continuity narrative; "no transition risk; same team,
  same tools, lessons learned."
- **Likely win themes:** customer continuity, ITSM maturity, named bench
  with active clearances on day one.
- **Genuine strengths on this bid:** zero transition risk; deep customer
  knowledge; existing staff.
- **Genuine vulnerabilities on this bid:** recent SLA breaches; perception
  of complacency; aging toolset.
- **Likely price posture:** competitive, possibly thin to defend the base.
